A session is a named workspace: *Work*, *Personal*, *Production*, *Side project*. Selecting a session swaps every tab and pane in the window for that session's own.

Use sessions instead of a pile of windows. You keep one window, and the context you need is one click away.

## Manage sessions

Sessions live at the top of the sidebar. You can create, rename, reorder and delete them there.

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| New session | <kbd>⌘⇧N</kbd> |
| Next / previous session | <kbd>⌘⌃]</kbd> / <kbd>⌘⌃[</kbd> |
| Go to session 1–9 | <kbd>⌘⌃1</kbd> … <kbd>⌘⌃9</kbd> |
| Show or hide the sidebar | <kbd>⌘0</kbd> |

> **Did you know?** The status bar always shows the current session's name, so you can tell where you are even with the sidebar hidden.

## Restore

Every window comes back on relaunch, in its place, with its sessions, tabs, splits and each pane's directory. See [Restore and windows](/docs/explore/restore/).
