Panes are detached cards with rounded corners and a gap between them. The focused pane gets an accent border, and each has a header with its title and split, zoom and close buttons.

## Split and focus

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Split right | <kbd>⌘D</kbd> |
| Split down | <kbd>⌘⇧D</kbd> |
| Focus pane | <kbd>⌘⌥</kbd> + arrow |
| Zoom pane | <kbd>⌘⇧↩</kbd> |
| Equalize panes | <kbd>⌘⌃=</kbd> |
| Close pane | <kbd>⌘W</kbd> |

You can also click a pane to focus it.

## Resize

Drag the gap between two panes to resize them. **Double-click the gap** to equalize.

## Zoom

<kbd>⌘⇧↩</kbd> makes the focused pane fill the tab. The other panes keep running in the background. Press it again to restore the layout.

> **Did you know?** The status bar shows the pane count when a tab is split, and the grid size of the focused pane such as `112×28`. That is handy when a program asks for your terminal size.

Splits are restored on relaunch and after updates.
