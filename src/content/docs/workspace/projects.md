---
title: Projects
description: "Save folders in the sidebar and open a new tab in any of them with one click."
order: 2
---

Projects are folders you visit often. They sit under **Sessions** in the sidebar.

- **Add one** by dragging a folder from Finder onto the list, or with the `+` button.
- **Open one** by clicking it. Jelly opens a new tab in that folder, in the current session.

> **Tip.** Pair projects with sessions: keep a *Work* session for client repos and a *Personal* session for side projects, and use Projects as the quick-launch list for both.

A per-project `jelly.toml` that opens your usual tabs and splits together is on the [roadmap](/docs/project/roadmap/).
